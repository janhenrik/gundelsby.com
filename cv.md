## Summary
Head of Knowit Research and part-time Lecturer in Software Engineering at the University of Oslo. I study how organizations adopt AI agents in software development, and help them do it. Bridging academia and practice on human-AI collaboration, agentic software engineering, technical debt, and large-scale agile.

Over 30 years as a developer, architect, and technology leader, from microservices and DevOps in Oslo municipality to building and running agentic development setups today. IT strategy, technical leadership, mentoring, and lifelong learning for consultants.

Published researcher on agile teams, human-AI collaboration, and technical debt. Regular speaker at JavaZone, XP, and other venues – see [talks](/foredrag).

Technical specialties:
- **Agentic software engineering:** adopting and scaling AI coding agents in teams, human-in-the-loop workflows, and harness and context engineering (skills, agent instructions, evaluation)
- **AI adoption research:** following organizations through agent-driven change, maturity baselines, and measuring real effect beyond the hype
- **Technical and cognitive debt:** managing debt in codebases and teams, including the new kinds AI-generated code creates
- **Platforms and organization:** platform engineering, product organization, DevOps, and large-scale agile in large enterprises and the public sector
- **Cloud and data:** AWS and Databricks certified; microservices and integration architecture
